4

Get personalized action plans

For each flagged employee, Peoplera generates a manager-ready action plan — This Week and Next 2 Weeks — tailored to that person's specific signal pattern.

Plans are built from observable work-behavior signals and, optionally, anonymous consent-based CBI pulse-survey results — plus a behavioral pattern profile (e.g. sustained overload vs. boundary erosion).

IMPORTANT
Peoplera is a decision-support tool based on work-behavior signals. It does not analyze social media, private messages, or perform psychological diagnosis. Plans are advisory — always apply your own professional judgment.

Privacy & ethics are not an afterthought

Data privacy is the foundation, not a feature. Peoplera is built to help people — designed with the wellbeing of employees in mind.

🔒
Your data stays yours
Each company only ever sees its own team's data. Burnout scores are never visible to other companies. No data selling, no advertising.
🇪🇺
GDPR compliant, EU hosted
Data is processed under EU GDPR. Encrypted in transit and at rest. Hosted in the EU — no transatlantic transfers.
🛡️
Decision-support, not surveillance
Peoplera is designed to help people, not to punish or rank them. Signals are based on work behavior — never private data.
🇳🇱
Built for Dutch duty of care
Supports Dutch employers' obligation under the Arbeidsomstandighedenwet (Arbo wet) to monitor and prevent psychosocial work risks.
This is a tool built for people, in service of people.
