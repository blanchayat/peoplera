How we detect burnout early

Three layers of intelligence — built to catch burnout before it costs you.

01 — Workload & Vacation Signals

Automatic risk detection

Peoplera tracks weekly hours, after-hours messages, sick day patterns, and vacation gaps. Employees who haven't taken time off in 6+ months and work 55h+ weeks are flagged before they burn out.

See how it works →
02 — Weekly Check-in

5-question pulse survey

Every week, employees receive a short 5-question psychological check-in. Responses are anonymous, science-backed, and feed directly into their burnout score — giving you a signal no spreadsheet can catch.

See how it works →
03 — Team Imbalance

Who's carrying the load?

When one person does the work of three, the whole team suffers. Peoplera maps workload distribution across your team and surfaces imbalance — so managers can act before someone breaks.

See how it works →

All three signals combine into one weekly Burnout Score — with a ready-to-use action plan.
